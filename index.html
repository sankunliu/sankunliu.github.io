About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Western University, advised by Prof. Yimin Yang. My current research focuses on coreset methods, model optimization, time series analysis, and recommendation systems.

Before joining Western University, I worked on machine learning and data analysis in the IoT Department at Gree Electric Appliances. I received my M.Sc. in Control Science and Engineering from Zhengzhou University.
